## Fixed Issues:

## Controls:
- **Left Mouse Button** - Shoot current weapon
- **1 Key** - Switch to Pistol
- **2 Key** - Switch to Pump Shotgun  
- **3 Key** - Switch to Grenade
- **4 Key** - Switch to Flashbang
- **G Key** - Toggle between Grenade and Flashbang

## Weapon Types:
1. **Pistol** - Fast firing, accurate
2. **Pump Shotgun** - Spread shot, 5 pellets
3. **Grenade** - Thrown explosive (5 second lifetime)
4. **Flashbang** - Tactical flash (4 second lifetime)

## How to Test:
1. Run `./run.sh`
2. Select "Play Game" from menu
3. Use number keys 1-4 to switch weapons
4. Use LEFT MOUSE to shoot
5. Press G to toggle between Grenade/Flashbang
